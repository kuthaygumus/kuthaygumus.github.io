Bloomind

Breathe Better, Feel Calmer

Bloomind is a general wellness app, not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical or mental-health care.

12 Breathing Techniques

Many techniques are paired with a plant in your garden.

Safety: never practice breathing techniques while driving, operating machinery, or in or near water. Sit or lie down, and stop if you feel lightheaded.

Calm & Relaxation
  • 4-7-8 Breathing — a slow rhythm to help you wind down before sleep
  • Coherent Breathing — a steady, even rhythm for calm
  • Pursed Lip — a gentle technique to help you unwind
  • Physiological Sigh — a double inhale and long exhale to reset fast
Focus & Energy
  • Box Breathing — used for mental clarity under pressure
  • Energizing 2-1-4 — a quick energy boost
  • Cold-Exposure Breathing — power breathing with breath retention for energy and focus
Calm in the Moment
  • Panic Seed — quick breathing for moments of overwhelm
  • Alternate Nostril — helps balance your breathing rhythm
Sleep
  • Deep Calm — extended exhale for bedtime relaxation
  • Moon Breathing — left-nostril breathing for a cooler, calmer bedtime
Advanced
  • Tummo — Tibetan inner-fire breathing for warmth and focus

Your Living Garden

Every breath plants a seed. Complete sessions to grow 12 unique plants from Seed to Full Bloom. Watch your garden grow as your practice develops.
