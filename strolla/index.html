Strolla

Japanese walking, coached out loud — with your phone locked in your pocket.

The walk

3 minutes brisk, 3 minutes easy, five times: the interval walking protocol from Shinshu University, 30 minutes a session. Strolla tells you when to speed up and when to ease off, so you never look at the screen. The method, explained.

Works when your phone is locked
Spoken cues keep coming with the screen off. Your music keeps playing and dips only for the cue.
Counts your brisk minutes
Every brisk minute banked, walk by walk. A crane companion grows with you.
Apple Health
With your permission, every walk you finish is saved as a Walking workout.
Private by design
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your walks stay on your device.

Strolla Premium

The standard walk, the spoken cues, your walk history and Apple Health saving are free. Premium adds trends across your walks, the Apple Watch app, and your own interval rhythm.
